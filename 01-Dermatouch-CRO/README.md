# Dermatouch | CRO & Growth Work

## Overview

Built and contributed to Dermatouch's CRO and growth capability across funnel diagnostics, checkout optimisation, experimentation, revenue-risk identification and growth opportunity discovery.

The work focused on moving beyond surface-level conversion metrics to identify the underlying causes of performance changes and prioritise interventions based on measurable business impact.

## Selected Areas of Work

### Funnel & Conversion Diagnostics
Analysed CVR movement across SKUs, campaigns and customer cohorts to distinguish between traffic-quality issues, product-page friction and checkout failures.

### Checkout & Revenue Risk
Investigated payment and COD flows, identifying reliability gaps, friction points and opportunities for revenue protection and recovery.

### Experimentation
Built a structured experimentation approach covering hypothesis development, success metrics, runtime planning and test decision-making.

### Competitive & Growth Research
Evaluated competitive experiences and emerging acquisition opportunities to identify gaps across discovery, conversion and customer experience.
