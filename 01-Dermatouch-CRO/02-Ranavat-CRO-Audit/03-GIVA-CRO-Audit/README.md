# GIVA | Independent CRO Audit

## Scope

An independent, outside-in CRO audit of GIVA's website experience, focused on customer intent, discovery friction, trust and checkout conversion opportunities.

## Approach

The audit combined:

- Quantitative opportunity framing
- Simulated first-time customer journeys
- Qualitative observation
- Heuristic evaluation using the LIFT Model
- ICE-based prioritisation
- Test design and measurement planning

## Key Focus Areas

- Homepage and category discovery
- Gifting and customer-intent alignment
- App-to-web conversion friction
- Trust and domain consistency
- Prioritised experimentation opportunities

## Outcome

The audit translated observed friction points into prioritised recommendations and test concepts rather than relying solely on generic CRO best practices.
