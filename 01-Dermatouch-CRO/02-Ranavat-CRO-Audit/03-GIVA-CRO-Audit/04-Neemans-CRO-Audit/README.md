# Neeman's | CRO Audit

## Overview

A structured conversion audit evaluating opportunities across the customer journey.

The audit focused on identifying friction, hesitation and missed conversion opportunities, then translating observations into actionable recommendations using a structured CRO framework.

## Framework

Each opportunity was evaluated through:

- Issue identification
- Recommended intervention
- CRO rationale
- Customer behaviour and UX principles
- Expected conversion impact
- Prioritisation

## Objective

The objective was not simply to identify visual or UX issues, but to connect each recommendation to the behavioural or conversion mechanism it was expected to influence.
