
# Kemi Ghetia | CRO Portfolio

### Conversion Rate Optimization · Experimentation · D2C Growth

I am a CRO and Growth professional focused on identifying missed/leaked revenue opportunities, where and why customers drop off, translating behavioural and quantitative signals into clear hypotheses, and prioritising interventions based on business impact.

My work spans the intersection of **data, customer behaviour, UX and experimentation**—from diagnosing funnel leaks and checkout friction to building testing infrastructure and identifying revenue and growth opportunities.

## CRO/Growth Impact Snapshots

- Identified **₹1.03 Cr in revenue exposure** by uncovering a 94.8% bypass rate in a high-risk COD checkout control (based on user segmentation - risk score wise).
- Diagnosed a **₹4.09 Cr attribution visibility gap** affecting marketing decision-making, traced the underlying UTM and platform-mapping failures, solved visibility upto 80%.
- Identified a **₹5.3L/month revenue recovery opportunity** through COD pricing and RTO-risk analysis focusing on mid-risk users.
- Improved checkout performance by reducing cart abandonment by **3%** while CVR moved by **1%**.
- Built experimentation and diagnostic capabilities spanning **A/B testing, hypothesis structuring, funnel RCA and automated customer-journey QA**.
- Identified an entire **new growth acquisition channel** through structured competitor research and internal gaps, recommended and implemented low effort, high impact ship, now a growing pipeline for revenue.

## How I Approach CRO

**Data → Customer Insight → Diagnosis → Hypothesis → Experiment → Learning**

I do not view CRO as a collection of isolated website “best practices.” My approach is to identify the underlying customer or business problem, connect quantitative and qualitative evidence along with heuristic evaluation, and design interventions that can produce both measurable impact and reusable learning.
